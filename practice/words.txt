The lighthouse keeper woke before the sun, as he always did.
He climbed the stairs, all one hundred and twelve of them, and checked the lamp.
The lamp was fine. The lamp was always fine, but he checked it anyway.

Outside, the sea was grey and restless. A gull landed on the rail, looked at him,
and decided he was not worth the trouble. "Fair enough," said the keeper.

He made tea. He drank the tea. He wrote in the log: "Sea grey. Lamp fine. Gull rude."
Then he sat by the window and watched the boats, and the boats did not watch him back.
